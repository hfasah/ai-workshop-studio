1-Minute Lesson Script: What Is MCP?

Think of an AI model as a talented employee. It can reason, write, and analyze—but without access to your business tools, it cannot do much in the real world.

That is where MCP, or Model Context Protocol, comes in.

MCP is a standard way for AI systems to securely connect with external tools and information—such as Google Drive, Slack, databases, calendars, CRMs, and internal business applications.

Instead of building a different custom connection for every AI model and every tool, developers can use one common protocol. Think of MCP as a universal adapter for AI.

Through MCP, an AI agent can find a document, check a customer record, query a database, or schedule a meeting—depending on the permissions it has been given.

MCP does not make AI automatically trustworthy or secure. Organizations still need authentication, access controls, approvals, monitoring, and audit logs.

The key takeaway is simple: the model provides the intelligence, MCP provides the connection, and your guardrails provide the control.
