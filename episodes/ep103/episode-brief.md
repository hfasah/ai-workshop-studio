# Episode brief: ep103

Title: Machine Learning vs Generative AI
Series: AI With Hippolyte · AI Fundamentals · Lesson 3 of 10
Format: build story (target 3 to 3.5 minutes; series rule 2 to 4 minutes)
Audience: business owners, managers, job seekers and builders establishing a common AI foundation
Learning goal: Machine learning predicts labels or numbers from your past examples; generative AI produces content from the world's text. Different tools for different questions.

## Story
User need: Ndedi wants to know each morning which deliveries will run late. An ML model trained on six months of history predicts risk; generative AI explains it.
Setting: Douala, Cameroon (Littoral region). Wouri Express, a small delivery company, is fictional. Documented conditions used: port gate queues, rainy-season flooding on the Bonabéri side, road checkpoints. No statistics are claimed.
Cast: Mbella (owner), Ndedi (dispatcher), Ekambi and Moukoko (drivers). All fictional. Names to be confirmed by Hippolyte at gate 3.
Running thread: the Friday delay report. Each lesson adds one capability to the same system, from sorting messages (lesson 1) to the full rule/AI/person pipeline (lesson 10).
Story status: fictional teaching scenario, declared on the title scene and in the description.

## Safety angle
A prediction is a probability, not a fact; routes with no history return not enough data; no driver is scored.
