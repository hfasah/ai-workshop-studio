## Issues (scene id · problem · exact correction)

1. **listening, decide, decide-flow (agency)** · The story brief says Njume sets the rule "answers only from the sheet, every product or dose question comes to me" and Ebot sets "ask about my farm first and say where the answer comes from". In the script those rules come from the crew: tanyi ("Ours asks about her farm first...") and the gatekeeper ("One hard rule..."). Neither Ebot nor Njume is shown asking for them, so the crew decides what AI should and should not do for two people who, in the brief, decided it themselves. This is the one saviour-framing risk in the episode. · Correction, `listening.onScreen.steps[1].ui.messages`: add a third Njume message `{"from": "agent", "text": "Njume: Anything about products or doses comes to me, not from a machine."}`. `listening.onScreen.steps[0].ui.messages`: add a second Ebot message `{"from": "user", "text": "Ebot: Ask me about my farm before you answer. And tell me where the advice comes from."}`. Then attribute in the spoken lines: amara line 2 → "Njume, the only field agent, keeps a checked guidance sheet. His rule: products and doses come to him, not from a machine." (26 words); decide, tanyi line 2 → "Ebot's rule: ask about her farm first. Njume's rule: answer only from his sheet, and show the source."; decide, gatekeeper → "Njume's hard rule becomes ours. No product name, no dose, ever comes from the model. Those go to him."

2. **listening (framing)** · tanyi: "No context about her farm, no source. We fix that." "We fix that" makes the crew the fixer of Ebot's problem; the standard is that the crew builds to the users' spec. · Correction: "No context about her farm, no source. Ebot and Njume told us what they need. We build to that."

3. **listening (voice: Njume reads like a brief, not a field agent)** · "I am one agent for many farms." and "I keep a checked guidance sheet." are the story brief's wording in a chat bubble; "checked guidance sheet" is builder vocabulary. · Correction, `listening.onScreen.steps[1].ui.messages`: message 1 → "Njume: I cover all the member farms alone. Many farmers around here never see an extension officer." (keeps B8 in no-figure form); message 2 → "Njume: The cooperative has an advice sheet I keep up to date. Questions reach me faster than I can reach the farms." Keep "guidance sheet" as the crew's and the UI's name for it (build, user-test, ownership); only Njume's own bubbles change.

4. **hook (hardship cue and inconsistency)** · "A chatbot on a borrowed phone": the borrowed phone is unsourced fiction (story brief, B11 has no figure) and reads as a poverty cue; it also contradicts the rest of the episode, where Ebot messages the cooperative and the queue lives "on the phone". The word carries no lesson. · Correction: spoken line 2 → "A chatbot on her phone answers: spray product X at dose Y. Nobody asked where her farm is." The `onScreen.text` needs no change.

5. **constraint-outage (gate decision: one outage line)** · The 2017 fact appears three times in one scene: the spoken line, `steps[0].detail` ("2017: 3 months without internet in the two English-speaking regions") and `steps[0].ui.lines[0]` ("2017: the two English-speaking regions lost the internet for 3 months"). The tool must not."; `steps[0].ui.lines` → ["Documented: months without internet in this region (2017)", "Design rule: it must work when the network does not"]. One statement on screen, one spoken.
