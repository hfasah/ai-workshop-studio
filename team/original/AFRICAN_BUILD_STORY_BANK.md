# African AI Build Story Bank

These are fictional starting points, not claims of completed deployments. Before using one, the Researcher and African AI Story Director must select and verify a specific country, community, market, language, regulatory setting, and user workflow. Local users and domain professionals should shape the solution.

| Build story | People using it | What characters build | AI lesson | Reality that changes the design |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Farmer advisory | Smallholder farmers and agronomists | Multilingual crop-support assistant with expert escalation | RAG, confidence, tools, human-in-the-loop | Connectivity, local crop data, feature phones, harmful advice risk |
| Classroom tutor | Students and teachers | Curriculum-aligned tutor supporting an African language | Prompting, retrieval, evaluation | Teacher control, curriculum, language data, device sharing |
| Rural clinic support | Nurses and clinicians | Documentation and referral assistant | Classification, summarization, safeguards | Privacy, clinical validation, limited connectivity, escalation |
| Market business assistant | Traders and small businesses | Voice-first inventory and bookkeeping helper | Speech AI, structured extraction, automation | Noise, literacy, languages, affordability, trust |
| Job opportunity navigator | Job seekers and employers | Skills-to-opportunity matching and coaching system | Embeddings, ranking, bias evaluation | Unequal data, credential variation, scams, human review |
| Government document service | Residents and public servants | Form guidance and case-routing assistant | Tool use, workflow automation, audit logs | Language access, identity, due process, records security |
| Power reliability monitor | Technicians and energy users | Equipment anomaly and maintenance assistant | Time-series detection, prediction, alerts | Sensor coverage, false alarms, maintenance capacity |
| Flood warning service | Residents and emergency teams | Localized risk alerts and response dashboard | Data fusion, forecasting, uncertainty | Sparse sensors, last-mile communication, public trust |
| Local logistics planner | Drivers, merchants, and dispatchers | Route and delivery coordination assistant | Optimization plus AI agents | Address quality, changing roads, fuel cost, offline operation |

## Episode engine

For each story, show this sequence:

1. A person encounters a concrete problem in an existing workflow.
2. The Workshop Crew listens to users and a local domain specialist.
3. They define what AI should and should not do.
4. They build a small usable version.
5. A realistic constraint or failure appears.
6. They improve the data, interface, architecture, guardrail, or operating process.
7. A user tries the improved system.
8. The episode identifies who maintains it, owns the data and IP, receives value, and remains accountable.
9. Viewers receive one practical action or build challenge.

The story should teach an AI concept through the build. The product is not a magical solution, and an animation must not imply measured impact unless evidence exists.
