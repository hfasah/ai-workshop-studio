# AI With Hippolyte: The AI Workshop — Animation Team

## Mission

Create accurate, memorable, production-ready animated AI lessons that build on Hippolyte Asah's *Africa's AI Moment*: Africans move from consumers of technology to creators of useful systems, companies, research, datasets, infrastructure, and intellectual property.

The series follows African characters identifying real needs and building things people use. Its learning arc is **Understand AI → Recognize Africa's opportunity → Take action**, guided by the BUILD framework: Build capability, Use AI to solve African problems, Invest in African innovation, Lead responsibly, and Develop African ownership.

The recurring Workshop Crew should collaborate with local users and domain specialists. They do not arrive as outside saviours. The user problem and local expertise shape what gets built, while the crew makes the AI concepts visible through design, prototyping, testing, failure, improvement, and deployment decisions.

## Approval gates

1. **Brief approved:** audience, objective, duration, platform, and call to action.
2. **Story approved:** African setting, real user need, artifact being built, local constraints, agency, ownership, and authenticity risks.
3. **Script approved:** facts, voice, dialogue, and personal experience.
4. **Storyboard approved:** visual direction, character use, contextual accuracy, new assets, and estimated cost.
5. **Preview approved:** factual, educational, audiovisual, accessibility, continuity, and African-authenticity review.
6. **Publication approved:** Hippolyte explicitly authorizes external posting.

## Standard episode package

- `episode-brief.md`, `evidence-pack.md`, and `claim-ledger.csv`

- `quality-report.md`
- master 16:9 video, vertical 9:16 cut, captions, thumbnail, metadata, and source log

Use `AFRICAN_BUILD_STORY_BANK.md` for fictional starting points. Every selected concept still requires setting-specific research and authenticity review.

## Operating boundary

The team may research, draft, plan, generate previews, and prepare publication packages when authorized. It may not invent Hippolyte's experiences, clone unauthorized voices, incur material generation costs, or publish externally without explicit permission.
