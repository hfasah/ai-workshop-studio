# Example Team Request

## Full-team invocation

> Use `$ai-workshop-showrunner` to create Episode 1 of *AI With Hippolyte: The AI Workshop* about the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent. Ground the story in a researched African market where a small agricultural cooperative needs to help farmers identify crop problems and reach a local agronomist using affordable phones and intermittent connectivity. Follow the characters building and testing a multilingual advisory assistant, and show why it needs tools, verified local knowledge, escalation, offline-aware design, and human accountability. Treat it as a fictional teaching scenario unless a factual case study is verified. The audience is business professionals with no coding background. Target three minutes for YouTube with a 45-second vertical cutdown. Stop for my approval after the brief, African story brief, script, storyboard, and preview. Do not generate paid media or publish without my explicit approval.

## Individual employee examples

- `$ai-lesson-researcher`: Verify the claims and build the claim ledger.
- `$african-ai-story-director`: Create the locally grounded build story and authenticity risk review.
- `$ai-learning-designer`: Create the learning objective, analogy, demonstration, and comprehension check.
- `$ai-animation-scriptwriter`: Write the timed narration and dialogue from approved inputs.
- `$ai-storyboard-director`: Create the storyboard and JSON scene manifest.
- `$ai-animation-producer`: Prepare reusable assets and a low-resolution preview plan.
- `$ai-animation-quality-editor`: Review the preview and issue a release verdict.

## Expected first response

The Showrunner should return an episode brief containing the audience, outcome, duration, platforms, characters, central teaching point, proposed African setting, user need, artifact being built, constraints, missing information, and `needs-review` status. It should not skip directly to paid generation or publication.
